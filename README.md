# BopIt Sword
#### Made by: Connor Marsh, William Muckelroy III, and Olivia Sobek
For the class Junior Design in the University of Pittsburgh Electrical and Computer Engineering department, we were tasked with designing and building a toy that plays like a BopIt, but it could be of any form we wanted.

We decided to make a lifesize replica of the Flamesword from The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild.
![](images/design/IMG_9561.PNG)
The sword calls out certain actions like "swing left", or "stab" and detects your motions to determine if you did it correctly. The sword also hides an LED strip under the translucent acrylic tiles, allowing it to glow beautifully when you perform actions correctly.
